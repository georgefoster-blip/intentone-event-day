The day at a glance

Why this day matters

IntentOne is the only product we sell, and every client conversation, renewal, referral and interview rests on how well the whole company can describe one thing. The day is built around three things, in this order: learning, then competition, then connection. Nobody spends the day being talked at — everyone leaves having used, built with or explained the product.

The IntentOne Opportunity Challenge

Teams build against the IntentOne challenge and submit before the deadline. Submissions are public to everyone in the room, and the judging pass shortlists the strongest against the criteria in the brief.

The brief

Format

Company-wide group work in mixed teams, drawn across functions and offices, with remote colleagues placed into teams rather than watching. Each team takes a challenge from the menu and builds something against it during the morning block — around 150 minutes in parallel with the persona walkthroughs, including 50 minutes of focused build time before the shared window.

Groups and presentations

Two groups in London, one in Barcelona, one in Tel Aviv. New York does its group work after the shared window and enters a recorded pitch, judged overnight with the rest. At 15:00 London time every other group presents what they built live, with the whole company watching.

Judging

Shortlisting is AI first: submissions from this page are read against the challenge and the strongest are shortlisted (see the Shortlist tab). A human panel then picks the winners. Entries are judged overnight and the winners announced the following day.

Sales Dragon's Den · shared window, 13:00 London

Format. Individual pitches of three to five minutes, one person at a time, to a judging panel playing a CEO, a CTO and a CFO.
Who takes part. Around seven of the sales team pitch; everyone else watches live online, wherever they are.
What comes out. The company hears IntentOne pitched seven ways in one sitting. The point is product understanding for the room, not a sales ranking.
